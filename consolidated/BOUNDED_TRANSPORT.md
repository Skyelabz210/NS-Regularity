# Bounded-scale velocity transport with residue certificates

The [rational velocity scheme](EXACT_VELOCITY_LIMIT.md) uses an exact solenoidal intermediate at every step. Its common denominator can grow with repeated solves. The encoding below retains one selected scale $A$ as the state denominator, preserves centered incompressibility and total momentum, and bounds the resulting error independently of the grid size. It applies to the nearest-neighbour diffusion and the [mock theta stencil](CRAM_MOCK_THETA_TRANSFER.md), which has the coercivity and consistency used in the weak-limit proof.

## State and exact constraints

Let $D^0_j f(i)=f(i+e_j)-f(i-e_j)$ denote the raw centered difference in this note. Let $H$ average on the orbits of the two-step translations. There is one orbit on odd grids and eight on even grids. For a rational centered-divergence-free velocity $b$, solve for the mean-free vector potential on those orbits by

$$p=\operatorname{curl}_0\Big(-\sum_j(D^0_j)^2\Big)^\dagger b,\qquad b=\operatorname{curl}_0p+Hb.$$

Here the inverse is zero on the kernel. The decomposition follows from $\operatorname{curl}_0^2=\operatorname{grad}_0\operatorname{div}_0-\sum_j(D_j^0)^2$, which follows by commutation of translations. The kernel consists of fields constant on the orbits. The potential and the orbit means are rational by finite-dimensional rational linear algebra. The reference implementation reuses the rational Green kernel, with two-step wraparound on both odd and even grids.

Round each coordinate of $Ap$ to its nearest integer $P$ with a fixed tie convention. For each component, round the orbit means of $Ab$ to integers $J$ preserving their sum. The preserved total is possible if $Aq\overline b_j\in\mathbb Z$ for each velocity component, where $q=1$ or $8$ is the number of orbits. Their sizes are equal. To round, take the floors and add one at the sites with the largest fractional parts until the sum is reached. The sum of the fractions is integral. If it is positive it is smaller than the number of positive fractions, so every error is strictly smaller than one before dividing by $A$. The unforced exact velocity step preserves $\overline b$, so initializing the state with this mean compatibility suffices for every subsequent step. Initial data with other means can use a scale-compatible approximation converging to the prescribed mean.

The stored integer velocity is $U=\operatorname{curl}_0P+J$ with amplitude $U/A$. It has zero centered divergence since centered derivatives commute and each harmonic component is constant on its two-step orbit. The integer vorticity is $W=\operatorname{curl}_0U$, with physical vorticity $NW/(2A)$. For an arbitrary rational initial solenoidal field, the argument also applies after replacing its global mean by the closest compatible mean. Lean checks the divergence identity for this representation on any commutative additive translation group.

Each velocity component of the curl error is a sum of four signed potential errors, of size at most $1/(2A)$ each. The curl error has zero sum on every two-step orbit. It is therefore orthogonal in the cell inner product to the harmonic rounding error, whose components are bounded by $1/A$. With normalized measure,

$$\big\|U/A-b\big\|_{2,h}^2\le 3(4+1)A^{-2},\qquad \big\|U/A-b\big\|_{2,h}<4/A.$$

The scale $A$ stays fixed through an entire run on one mesh. The potential implementation still uses exact rational intermediate solves. It provides a certified storage and update boundary for a residual-controlled solver. The dense exact solve is a reference algorithm, with no production performance claim.

## Velocity estimate and nonlinear weak limit

Let $a^n$ be the stored velocities in physical units and $b^{n+1}$ the implicit output from $a^n$ before encoding. Set $\epsilon=4/A$. Energy gives $\|b^{n+1}\|_{2,h}\le\|a^n\|_{2,h}$. Encoding gives $\|a^{n+1}-b^{n+1}\|_{2,h}\le\epsilon$. On a grid horizon of $m$ steps we therefore have $M=\|a^0\|_{2,h}+m\epsilon$ as a common norm bound. Squaring the two endpoint norms about $b^{n+1}$ incurs at most $2M\epsilon$. Thus the kinetic energy identity gives

$$\|a^m\|_{2,h}^2+\sum_{n<m}\|b^{n+1}-a^n\|_{2,h}^2+2\nu_h\sum_{n<m}\tau\|D^+b^{n+1}\|_{2,h}^2\le\|a^0\|_{2,h}^2+2Mm\epsilon.$$

This inequality also holds on any grid subinterval using the same $M$. The energy adjustment is proved in Lean from the norm and one-step energy bounds. For a smooth-reference comparison, the [one-step stability bound](NS_GAP_ANALYSIS.md) picks up the additional $\epsilon$ at each step. At fixed $T$ its accumulated error is at most $\mathrm e^{LT}(T/\tau)\epsilon$. Choosing $1/(A\tau)=O(h^2+\tau)$ preserves the matched-nodal rate when the other hypotheses hold.

For general finite-energy data choose $A$ so that $1/(A\tau)\to0$ through refinement. Assume the initial stored velocities converge in cell $L^2$ to the prescribed divergence-free datum. The preceding estimate bounds the intermediate gradients and the jump sum, with vanishing cumulative energy defect. The stored right endpoints and intermediates differ by at most $\epsilon$ in cell $L^2$. The time-integrated squared difference between the left endpoints and intermediates is bounded by $\tau$ times the jump sum. In the fixed-Fourier-mode estimate of the [original limit](EXACT_VELOCITY_LIMIT.md#3-passage-to-the-continuum), the variation of a stored coefficient now has the additional per-step $\epsilon$. Because $\epsilon/\tau\to0$, the piecewise-linear mode interpolants remain equi-Lipschitz. The spatial tails of the intermediates are controlled by the energy bound and transfer to the stored endpoints through their $L^2$ difference. The same diagonal compactness proof gives strong space-time $L^2$ convergence of both endpoints and the intermediates, up to a subsequence. Against a smooth test, replacing $b^{n+1}$ with $a^{n+1}$ in the discrete time difference costs at most $m\epsilon$ times the maximum test norm, which tends to zero. The nonlinear flux product still converges strongly in $L^1$. The subinterval energy estimates have vanishing defects, so the Leray–Hopf inequality passes to the limit as before. The argument concerns this encoded rational velocity scheme, whose exactness refers to the carried representation and whose approximation is the measured change at the encoding boundary.

## CRAM carrier and signed windows

Choose pairwise coprime moduli $m_i=p_i^{e_i}$ from the safe-basis prime families $p_i\in\{2,3,5,7,11,13\}$. The primary prime lanes stay fixed. Higher powers provide precision in those families. Let $M_{\mathrm{CRT}}=\prod_i m_i$. If a scalar has a certified signed numerator bound $B$ with $2B<M_{\mathrm{CRT}}$, then the map from the signed window to these residues is injective. Equal residues imply divisibility of the difference by every $m_i$, hence by their product. The difference has magnitude at most $2B$. Lean checks the final signed-window implication. The verifier validates a supplied numerator by comparing residues, with no Garner reconstruction and no stored winding.

A rigorous per-state range is available from the energy bound. The stored velocity numerator satisfies $|U_j(i)|\le A h^{-3/2}M$, and $|W_j(i)|\le4A h^{-3/2}M$ by the raw centered curl. An integer ceiling or a sharper independently checked interval can be used. The fixture checks the signed window at every stored step for both fields. Arithmetic before the encoding boundary still needs range bounds for each intermediate on a modular implementation. For a nonunit denominator, keep its positive scale outside the value lanes and apply a certified exact rescaling when needed. The existing [Theory of the One adapter](bridges/theory_observations/NSObservations.lean) reads the quotient of a genuine numerator from adjacent observations within the signed window. It gives a division certificate when the operation and its numerator are specified. Compactness and viscosity control come from the estimates above.

## Reproduce

Run `python3 scripts/verify_bounded_transport.py` from the repository root. The reference checks bounded encoding on $N=3,4,6,8$ and three full nonlinear solve-and-encode steps with mock theta viscosity at $N=3$ and nearest-neighbour viscosity at $N=4$. It records each exact solve's denominator size, verifies the fixed storage scale and displays the largest carried numerator. `lake build` under `lean/` checks the finite divergence, rounding, window and energy statements. The compactness and convergence arguments are written proofs.
