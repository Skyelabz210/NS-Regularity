## Architecture corrections received during session

### 1. Lanes hold ENTIRE EQUATIONS, not atomic ops
The 32,768 schemas counted are choices among atomic operators. The actual
NS schema search uses entire physics terms loaded into lanes:
```
Σ = (L, P, D, S, B, K, C, M, T)
{P, T, D, S, K, H, A, R, E, M}
```

### 2. NormForm/Legendre/Frobenius are CHIMERA operators
Lane operators take (a_i, b_i) → c_i within Z/p_i Z. Chimera operators
read multiple lanes / change what the lane ring IS. Cannot be added to
`CramOp` enum directly.

### 3. CRTBigInt stacks, Recumbent overflow tiers wrap around it
If implementations are used through, magnitude problems disappear.
Truncation issues come from leaving residue space (calling `to_i64()`
in inner loops). The discipline:
- Stay in residues
- Use K-Elim or FPD for division
- CRTBigInt stacks (8 lanes, 2^504 capacity)
- Recumbent wraps with Primary/Overflow/Hot tiers

### 4. "Composite of 2 (ie 1) then prime power it"
Substrate's moduli aren't hand-picked primes coprime to SCALE.
EXACT_MODULI are 2^63 minus tiny offsets — 2-tower lifted to power 63.
SCALE = 10⁴ = 2⁴·5⁴ isn't an obstacle to division — it's the
Recumbent's `primary_radius` corridor boundary. Division by SCALE is
TIER MIGRATION not arithmetic.

### 5. Normalization = truncation in different language
Renormalize-divide-by-max IS integer truncation. The "scale-invariant"
framing dressed up integer truncation in different language.

## The Push 4 vs Push 6 contradiction

Same equation, same dt, same backgrounds. Push 4 measured 0.955
contraction. Push 6 measured ~10⁹ amplification. **10⁹× different.**

Root cause identified: the two probes use **different BiotSavart
implementations with different scaling conventions**.

| Probe   | BiotSavart                       | u-magnitude |
|---------|----------------------------------|-------------|
| Push 4  | `nsgrid3d::BiotSavartNTT::apply` (denom=N⁶) | ~10⁴ |
| Push 5/6| `ns_v5::ns3d::biot_savart_n8`              | ~5×10⁶ |

Factor ~500× difference in u. Bilinear terms ~500× per term, compound
across multiple terms and steps to 10⁹× discrepancy.

Additional: Push 4's hand-written `cross_advection_3d` uses
`(u·Δω)/SCALE`; canonical `discrete_advection` uses `(u·Δω)·N/(2·SCALE)`.
Factor 4× at N=8.

**The workspace has two BiotSavart conventions that aren't
interchangeable.** This is the meta-architectural finding.
